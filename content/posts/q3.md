Here are the good things that have happened to me in the last 3 months.

## Pharmaceuticals

Around 2 months ago, I learned that there is actually One Weird Trick to eliminate all my personal addictions overnight.

I don't use it that often, because my addictions have become part of my daily life and I'm loath to stop.

But it is fairly comforting to have a solution available as a quick discrete choice.

## Lack of brain damage

On many occasions over the last few months, I felt I experienced one-sided numbness, facial drooping, intense headpain, reading difficulties... basically every symptom in [this list](https://www.healthhub.sg/well-being-and-lifestyle/personal-care/blow-to-the-brain#:~:text=Stroke%3A%20About%20Stroke-,Spot%20the%20Signs%20of%20a%20Stroke,-!) bar vision loss.

However, repeated MRI scans have failed to identify any damage. All LLMs agree I have no restricted diffusion, no intracranial haemorrhage, no hydrocephalus or midline shift, etc.

This necessarily implies I have not experienced any significant long term brain damage, which is great. I'm not sure what the future will bring, but I can be reasonably confident I haven't experienced any serious injuries this year.

## Social activities

Insofar as I participated in any offline social activities, they were broadly enjoyable.

I think [this take](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T1g0ely6jFs) is mostly correct. The peak of human life is in human interaction, not manifesting Plato's shadows on the internet. Especially when that firewood is now made of silicon.

## Basic foundations

For much of my life, I lacked the mental wherewithal to do the meticulous things: meal prep, room clearing, digital security, home server, financial planning, utility/replacement items, etc.

These things have all gradually improved over the year. Things are still quite wrong, but they are much less wrong than they used to be.

## Conclusion

Many good things have happened in the last quarter.
